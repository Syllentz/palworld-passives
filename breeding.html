How it works. Normal breeding comes from PalCalc's pair table, which is datamined from the Palworld 1.0 game files (all special combos, plus the gender-dependent Katress + Wixen pair). Wild levels come from the same data. Routes are ranked by effort: each breed counts as one step, catching gets much costlier with level (a Lv 1 Pal is half a step, a Lv 60 one about nine), and mutation steps get a penalty based on how many eggs you'd expect to need. Mutations (1% per egg, 3% with an Extravagant Vegetable Cake) hatch into a different, stronger species. The odds use paldb.cc's model, which paldb worked out from in-game testing, so treat them as close estimates. Mutated Pals hatch as Alphas with 90–100 IVs and rainbow passives.
